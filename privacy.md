# DocuWatch Privacy Policy

*Effective 27 October 2026*

Your documents stay on your iPhone.

## What we collect

Nothing. DocuWatch has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. We never receive your documents, your name or anything about how you use the app.

## What the app stores

The documents you add (holder name, type, country, expiry date, notes and renewal history) and your settings are saved on your iPhone only. If your iPhone uses iCloud Backup or a computer backup, iOS includes this data in that backup, protected by your Apple Account. We can't read it.

## Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a passport or ID card. The machine-readable lines are read on the device by Apple's Vision framework. No photos are saved or sent anywhere.

## Face ID and passcode

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. DocuWatch only learns whether the check succeeded and never sees biometric data.

## Reminders and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on the device by iOS. Widgets and notifications show document details on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. With the app lock on, they show only how soon something expires.

## Backups you export

A backup file is created only when you ask for one, and goes only where you choose: Files, AirDrop, Mail or another app. If you set a password, the file is encrypted with AES-256. Once shared, the file is handled by the app or service you chose.

## Purchases

DocuWatch Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. We receive no payment or personal details, only confirmation that Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account.

## Deleting your data

Delete documents in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Exported backup files stay where you saved them until you delete them.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date. For questions, email support@docuwatch.cc.
