# DocuWatch

Track passport, residence visa, Emirates ID, Iqama and other GCC document expiry dates, with reminders before each one runs out. Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no servers, no tracking.

## Support

Email **[support@docuwatch.cc](mailto:support@docuwatch.cc)** and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the DocuWatch version (Settings › About).

**Never send document numbers, scans or photos of your documents.** We don't need them to help you.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone. We never receive it. iOS includes it in your iCloud or computer backup if you use one.

**How do I move my documents to a new iPhone?**
Restoring your iPhone from a backup brings them across. You can also export a backup in Settings › Backup and restore it on the new phone.

**I forgot my backup password.**
Passwords are never stored, so an encrypted backup can't be opened without it. Export a new backup from the iPhone that still has your documents.

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Check that notifications are allowed for DocuWatch in the iPhone Settings app. Reminders arrive at 9:00 on the days before each expiry date.

**How do I restore my Pro purchase?**
In Settings › DocuWatch Pro, tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
